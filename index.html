Method

How I approach an operation

A consistent four-part loop applied across every account, from a 6-person call-center floor to a 50+ FTE managed service.

Diagnose

Analyze historical SLA and quality data to surface trends and root causes before they become client escalations.

Redesign

Rebuild the workflow, escalation path, or SOP — running RCA and "Quality Walk" reviews to remove manual, repetitive work.

Automate

Convert the redesigned process into Power Automate, Power Apps, or Excel Macro workflows so the fix holds without manual policing.

Govern

Track the result on live KPI dashboards and PMO-level frameworks, with weekly team feedback to keep performance on track.
